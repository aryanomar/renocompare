❓ Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Everything homeowners and contractors need to know about RenoCompare v7.0

1. How does the new Smart Area Budget Calculator help homeowners? +
Inside the Project Banner, homeowners can open the "🧮 Smart Area Budget Calculator", enter their Carpet Area (sq.ft.), and choose Economy (₹1,200/sqft), Premium (₹1,850/sqft), or Luxury (₹2,600/sqft) to compute and apply a realistic target budget in one click.
2. How do Itemized Cost Splits and Red-Flag Risk Badges protect consumers? +
Every proposal card automatically breaks down quotes into Materials (55%) • Labor & Civil (30%) • MEP/Design (15%) and flags quotes >15% below budget with a "⚠️ Lowball Risk: Verify Material Grades" warning or missing scope alerts.
3. How does the Counter-Offer & Price Negotiation workflow work? +
Click "🤝 Counter-Offer" on any proposal card to propose a revised price and timeline. Submitting places a live "💬 Counter-Offer Sent" badge on the card and automatically records the official offer inside the Admin-monitored Contractor Chat!
4. How does the Two-Way Rating system protect both Consumers and Contractors? +
Homeowners rate contractors on material quality and timeline adherence, while contractors rate homeowners on clear requirements and timely milestone approvals—and both rate the RenoCompare platform!
